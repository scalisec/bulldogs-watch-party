Watch Party

by Bench Boss Labs · Keyboard shortcuts

Quick tag: press a number the moment something happens. The clip starts a few seconds earlier and ends a few seconds later (set per category in Settings).

Exact clip: press I at the start of the play, O at the end, then the category number.

Score and periods: press P at each faceoff that starts or ends a period, and G / H for goals. Clips then know their period and the score.

Zoom first, then tag: a clip keeps the zoom you had when you tagged it.
